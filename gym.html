Connect Hevy

Log workouts in Hevy; they show up here with every set, PR and muscle group. You connect it once, on the server, so the key never reaches your phone.
  1. The Hevy API needs Hevy Pro. Copy your API key from hevy.com/settings?developer.
  2. In Vercel → Settings → Environment Variables add HEVY_API_KEY with that key, then redeploy.
  3. Open this page again. The first sync pulls about a year of workouts. After that, new ones come in whenever you open Fitness or tap ↻.
